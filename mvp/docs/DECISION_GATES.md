# Product decision gates tracked during implementation

Implementation must not convert unresolved business behavior into accidental product policy.

## Gate before Milestone 2 profile/verification freeze
- DR-019 verification evidence/states/review rules.
- DR-032 location/search source and exact city/location behavior.
- Confirm minimum profile fields against complete Figma.

## Gate before Milestone 3 staff/RBAC freeze
- DR-002 initial facility owner/admin bootstrap.
- DR-016 facility staff role catalog and whether custom bundles are allowed.
- DR-047 appointment/private-field visibility where staff access is affected.

## Gate before Milestone 4 affiliation/contracts freeze
- DR-013 contract lifecycle.
- DR-014 commercial authority.
- DR-015 effective terms/versioning.
- DR-042 affiliation lifecycle/initiation.

## Gate before Milestone 5 scheduling freeze
- DR-007 availability ownership.
- DR-008 exception precedence.
- DR-010 working-day semantics.
- DR-012 timezone policy.
- DR-043 booking horizon.
- DR-044 slot granularity.
- DR-045 buffers.
- DR-046 availability changes around existing appointments.

## Gate before Milestone 6 booking/appointment freeze
- DR-003 booking for another person.
- DR-004 final appointment lifecycle.
- DR-005 cancellation.
- DR-006 rescheduling identity/version behavior.
- DR-009 capacity.
- DR-049 historical financial snapshot policy.
- DR-050 currency/tax behavior.

## Gate before Milestone 8 notifications
- DR-023 channels, consent and event/channel matrix.
